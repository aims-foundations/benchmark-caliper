## Use Case

Damage assessment provoked by natural disasters in urban areas, specifically floods. The information available consist of images and video captures from social media and sent by the affected local population.

## Target Population

Emergency managers from the Pampas region in Argentina. They speak Rio Platense Spanish, are born and raised in the cities they serve. No particular gender, most likely man between 30 and 45 years old.

## Elicitation Responses

_No elicitation dialogue was conducted for this assessment. To mirror the manual expert study — in which the expert assesses the benchmark directly from the deployment tuple (benchmark, use case, target population) with no elicitation stage — the pipeline was run directly on the tuple above. The Use Case and Target Population are the sole deployment context._

## Dimension Priority Weights

_Not elicited. All six validity dimensions (IO, IC, IF, OO, OC, OF) are assessed on their own merits, with no pre-assigned priority weights — matching the manual study, where the expert forms their own view of dimension severity during the assessment rather than receiving weights as input._
