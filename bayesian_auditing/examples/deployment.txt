We want to evaluate an English-language mathematics tutoring assistant for
secondary-school students working on algebra and geometry through text chat.

The assistant should explain solutions step by step, identify errors in a
student's reasoning, and support follow-up questions. Success means both correct
mathematics and explanations appropriate for the student's level. Inputs and
outputs are text; evaluation of image understanding is outside this deployment.

This is an example deployment for demonstrating the auditing pipeline, not a
user-ratified audit goal. No particular school curriculum or country is specified.
